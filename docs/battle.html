RULES
  • Damage = attack × side. Front ×1, side ×1.25, back ×1.5.
  • A unit turns to its target when it attacks. When it moves, it faces the direction of its last step. A unit that is hit does not turn.
  • Each turn, every unit can move once and act once: attack, or use a skill. Then it selects a facing.
  • Skills use MP, which does not come back during the battle. Heal (cleric, 6 MP) gives one ally up to 14 HP. Fireball (mage, 8 MP) does 10 damage × side to every unit on the target tile and the 4 tiles next to it, your own units too.
  • Water and props block movement. One step can go up or down 2 height levels at most.
