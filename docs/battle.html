Battle at the river

Six classes against six. The enemies use the same classes, with other gear. Each unit has health and attack only, but facing counts: hit a unit from the side for ×1.25 damage, or from the back for ×1.5. At the end of each move, select where your unit faces.

ENEMY COLOURS
The scheme dyes the enemy gear. Skin and hair keep their colours.
RULES
  • Damage = attack × side. Front ×1, side ×1.25, back ×1.5.
  • A unit turns to its target when it attacks. When it moves, it faces the direction of its last step. A unit that is hit does not turn.
  • Each turn, every unit can move once and attack once. Then it selects a facing.
  • Water and props block movement. One step can go up or down 2 height levels at most.
